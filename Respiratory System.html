The Respiratory/Pulmonary System

Functions:

  • Gas Exchange (Oxygen and Carbon Dioxide)
  • Breathing
  • Sense of Smell
  • Sound Production
  • Protection (in nasal cavity)

The Different Parts

  • Upper Pulmonary System

  1. Nasal Cavity
    • Warms, Filters, and moisturizes air
    • Has coarse hair, mucus, and cilia to filter out dirt from air

  2. Oral Cavity
    • Supplements or replaces nasal cavity function
    • Used for sound production

  3. Pharynx/Throat
    • Passageway for materials (food, air, water, etc.)

  4. Epiglottis
    • Prevents food from entering the trachea by accident (basically it sorts the materials out)
    • Has to do with your gag reflex

  5. Larynx/Voice box
    • The main part used for sound production
    • Air basically passes and vibrates vocal chords contained within to produce sound

  • Lower Pulmonary System

  1. Trachea/Windpipe
    • Airway that connects your upper and lower airways (bridge)

  2. Lungs
    • Contains small airways, alveoli, and blood vessels (capillaries)
    • Covered in pleura (double-layered membrane) which protects it

  3. Bronchial Tubes
  4. Bronchi
  5. Bronchioles
    • The bronchial tree/structure (all parts) are small airways
    • Decrease in size in descending order

  6. Alveoli
    • Center for gas exchange

  7. Pulmonary Capillaries
    • Deliver gases to and from the alveoli to the circulatory system

  8. Diaphragm
    • Controls Breathing (expands when breathing; contracts when exhaling)

Mechanisms of Gas Exchange

  • Breathing
  • Transporting of gases to and from the respiratory system to the circulatory system
  • Supply the body cells (oxygen)
  • Produce energy (cellular respiration)

Homeostasis

  • The balance that the body needs to maintain to function properly (eg. Oxygen and Carbon dioxide, blood, temperature)

Body Disorders That Can Affect the Respiratory System

  1. Smoker's Lung (obviously)
  2. Having 1 Lung (due to pneumonectomy)
  3. Damaged/Paralyzed Diaphragm
  4. Obesity

A Bad Lifestyle That Affects the Respiratory System

  • Smoking

    • Causes smoker's lung which has side effects like decreased lung elasticity, increased risk of diseases, and cilia paralysis
    • Smoker's lung is caused by the harmful chemicals present in cigarette smoke
    • You can avoid this by switching to healthier coping mechanisms or seeking therapy
