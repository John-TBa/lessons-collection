Sum and Product of Roots

Definition:

  • The sum and product of the 2 roots that you get from solving a quadratic equation can be easily determined using 2 short formulas (Vieta's Formula)

  • Sum: - ba

  • Product: ca

(You just have to know the a, b, and c values, then substitute)

Examples:

  • x2 + 3x + 6 = 0
  1. a = 1, b = 3, c = 6
  2. Sum: - 31 = -3
  3. Product: 61 = 6

  • 3x2 + 6x + 9 = 0
  1. a = 3, b = 6, c = 9
  2. Sum: - 63 = -2
  3. Product: 93 = 3

Finding the Quadratic Equation Based on the Given Roots

Formula:

  • x2 - (s1 + s2)x + (s1 (s2)) = 0
  • To put it into simpler terms, x2 minus the sum of the roots multiplied by x, plus the product of the roots, is equal to 0

Examples:

  • (4, 3)
  1. s1 = 4, s2 = 3
  2. x2 - (4 + 3)x + (4 (3)) = 0
  3. x2 - 7x + 12 = 0

  • (3, -5)
  1. s1 = 3, s2 = -5
  2. x2 - (3 - 5)x + (3 (-5)) = 0
  3. x2 + 2 - 15 = 0
