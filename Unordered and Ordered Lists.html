Unordered and Ordered Lists

What's the Difference Between the Two?

  • Unordered Lists are items listed with bullet points and they are not ordered
  • Ordered Lists are items listed with a specific order (eg. numbers, letters, Roman numerals)

Demo:

Unordered List

Code: (ul) (li)Matthew(/li) (li)Luke(/li) (li)Mark(/li) (li)John(/li) (/ul)

What it actually looks like:

  • Matthew
  • Luke
  • Mark
  • John

You may also style it to make the bullet points into squares
Code: (ul style="list-style-type:square;") (li)Matthew(/li) (li)Luke(/li) (li)Mark(/li) (li)John(/li) (/ul)
  • Matthew
  • Luke
  • Mark
  • John
