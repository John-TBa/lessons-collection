The Different Eras of Literature

The Eras:

  1. Native American Era (Pre-Colonial)
    • Focuses on literature made through oral tradition (Poems, Epics, Legends, etc.) by the native Americans.
    • Most works were recovered through English accounts.

  2. Puritan Era (Colonial)
    • Characterized by the the Church of England's desire to "purify" the native Americans.
    • Literature focused on religion (specifically the Bible).

  3. Age of Enlightenment (American Revolution Era)
    • Characterized by the human nature to learn more about the world around them.
    • Literature in this era was mostly non-fictional (Eg. Declaration of Independence).

  4. Romanticism Era
    • Literature focused on dark supernatural themes (Eg. vampires).
    • Writers were called "Dark Romantics."
    • Most prominent figure at the time: Edgar Allan Poe

  5. Transcendentalism Era
    • Literature focused on individualism and the divinity of nature.
    • Most works were non-fiction

  6. Naturalism (Realism) Era
    • Literature focused on the social issue of discrimination.
    • Works included realistic elements.

  7. Modernism Era
    • Literature at this time went against the norms of writing.
    • Works did not follow the standard structure of plots (Exposition, Rising Action, Climax, Falling Action, Resolution). They were mostly non-linear.

  8. Harlem Renaissance (Jazz Age)
    • Golden age of Afro-American literature.
    • Literature included jazz and African American peoples' rights.
    • Afro-American Revolution

  9. Beat Generation (Post WWII)
    • Occured after WWII.
    • "Exhausted generation."
    • Literature during this time went against literary norms and had topics like sexual orientations.
    • Nonconforming creativity.
    • Literature went against war and promoted peace and sexuality.
