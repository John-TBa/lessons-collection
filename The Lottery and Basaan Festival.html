The Lottery by Shirley Jackson

Short Summary:

  • The story takes place in a seemingly normal village and a bright setting. As the story progresses, we start to learn more about the lottery, which is a traditional ritual done in that village. Based on how some of the characters were described as the story progressed, it was not an ordinary lottery. When Mrs. Hutchinson won the lottery, she started to plead with the villagers that it was not fair. After the villagers picked up some stones that were piled earlier, they stoned her to death.
  • Full story: The Lottery

Analysis:

  • The villagers were blindly following a tradition (Blind Obedience). Perhaps they did not know its true purpose.
  • The village already had the problem of overpopulation. The solution was to administer the lottery as a tradition.
  • The true winners of the lottery are the villagers because they have to distribute their resources to one less person, the "winner" who gets stoned to death is just a sacrifice.
  • In short, this story gives off the message to not give in to blind obedience. You have to question whether something is right or wrong, not just blindly follow it.
