Photosynthesis

General Equation: 6 CO2 + 12 H2O + Light Energy → C6H12O6 + 6 O2

What is its definition?

  • Photosynthesis is a biological process that occurs in plants (specifically their chloroplasts) which produces chemical energy.
  • It is divided into 2 processes, the light-dependent reactions which occur in the chloroplast's thylakoid, and the light-independent reactions which occur in the stroma.
  • Light-dependent reactions require light, while light-independent ones do not require light.

The different molecules involved:

  • NADPH (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide Phosphate) - High energy carrier of electrons
  • NADP+ - Also an electron carrier; has a lower energy level compared to NADPH due to being oxidized
  • H2O - Reactant of light-dependent reactions
  • O2 - Byproduct of light-dependent reactions
  • CO2 - Reactant of light-independent reactions
  • ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate) - Energy carrier that carries energy for cells
  • ADP - Used up version of ATP

How are the reactants absorbed?

  1. Water - Absorbed through the roots of the plant
  2. Light - Absorbed by the chloroplasts of the plant (specifically trapped by chlorophyll which is a green pigment; a pigment basically absorbs light energy)
  3. Carbon Dioxide - Absorbed through the stomata of a plant's leaf (The process releases water)

Light-Dependent Reactions

  • They are biological reactions in plants that require light.
  • Occurs in the thylakoid of a chloroplast.
  • This process releases oxygen as a byproduct.

  • Equation: H2O + Light Energy + ADP + NADP+ → O2 + ATP + NADPH

  • Reactants: H2O + Light Energy + ADP + NADP+

  • Products: O2 + ATP + NADPH

    • Absorbed light = Electricity which is used to convert water into oxygen (Electrolysis).

    Reactants are found at the tail end of the arrow (Left Side of the Equation), while the products are found at the arrowhead's end (Right Side of the Equation).


Light-Independent Reactions/Calvin-Benson Cycle/Dark Reaction

  • They are biological reactions in plants that do not require light.
  • Occurs in the stroma of a chloroplast.
  • Needs to undergo 2 cycles to produce a single glucose molecule.
  • This process produces organic material (Glucose) which could later be converted into energy (ATP) in cellular respiration.

  • Equation: CO2 + ATP + NADPH → C6H12O6 + ADP + NADP+

  • Reactants: CO2 + ATP + NADPH

  • Products: C6H12O6 + ADP + NADP+
