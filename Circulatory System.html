The Circulatory/Cardiovascular System

Functions

  • Transport oxygen throughout the body
  • Protect your body against infections and diseases
  • Transport waste products from the circulatory to the excretory system
  • Healing

The Different Components

  1. Blood
  2. Heart
  3. Blood Vessels

1. Blood

  • Produced in bone marrow
  • Stored in the spleen
  • Has 4 different components:

  1. Red Blood Cell (RBC)/Erythrocyte
    • Carries oxygen
    • Contains hemoglobin (a protein rich in iron that binds to oxygen) that makes it red
    • No nucleus
    • Has a biconcave shape
    • Diseases associated: Sickle cell anemia, Iron-deficiency anemia

  2. White Blood Cell (WBC)/Leukocyte
    • Fights bacteria and infections
    • Absorbs dead cells
    • Has a nucleus
    • Largest; no constant shape
    • Disease associated: Leukemia

  3. Platelet/Thrombocyte
    • Used for blood clotting
    • Forms scabs in wounds (basically plugs them)
    • No nucleus
    • Small; irregular shape

  4. Plasma
    • Yellow liquid; floats blood cells
    • 90% Water
    • Contains electrolytes, hormones, proteins, etc.

2. Heart

  • It is a muscular organ that pumps blood to the body
  • Vital organ
  • Divided into two parts: One carries deoxygenated blood, while the other carries oxygenated blood

The Different Parts

  • Right Side of the Heart (R-Dox/Deox Side)
  1. Superior Vena Cava
    • Carries Deox Blood from the upper parts of the body back to the heart

  2. Inferior Vena Cava
    • Carries Deox Blood from lower parts of the body

  3. Right Atrium
    • Collects Deox Blood

  4. Tricuspid Valve
    • Controls blood flow from R Atrium to R Ventricle

  5. Right Ventricle
    • Pumps Deox Blood to P Valve

  6. Pulmonary Valve
    • Controls blood flow from R Ventricle to P Artery

  7. Pulmonary Artery
    • Carries Deox Blood to lungs

  • Left Side of the Heart (L-Ox/Ox side)
  1. Pulmonary Vein
    • Carries Ox Blood from lungs to heart

  2. Left Atrium
    • Collects Ox blood

  3. Bicuspid Valve
    • Controls blood flow from L Atrium to L Ventricle

  4. Left Ventricle
    • Pumps Ox blood

  5. Aortic Valve
    • Controls blood flow from L Ventricle to Aorta

  6. Aorta
    • Carries Ox Blood away from the heart to the rest of the body


  • Other Parts of the Heart
  1. Septum
    • Divides the heart

  2. Pericardium
    • Protects and covers the heart

  3. Coronary Arteries
    • Supply blood to the heart

3. Blood Vessels

  • Act as a delivery system for blood
  • Control blood pressure
  • Exchange nutrients and waste between blood and cells
  • Redistribute blood to fit the body's needs (Homeostasis)
  • 3 different types:
  1. Artery
    • Carries Ox Blood away from the heart (except the pulmonary artery which carries Deox Blood)
    • High blood pressure
    • No valves
    • Thickest wall

  2. Vein
    • Carries Deox Blood back to the heart (except the pulmonary vein which carries Ox Blood)
    • Lowest blood pressure
    • Has valves
    • Thin walls; large lumen

  3. Capillary
    • Thinnest walls
    • Facilitates exchange of materials between blood and cells
    • No valves
